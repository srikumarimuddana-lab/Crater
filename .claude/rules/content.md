# Content boundaries

Use verified product, ingredient, usage, and market information. Mark preview
fixtures; never invent reviews, clinical evidence, certifications, or policies.
Generate structured data, canonical metadata, prices, and availability from shared
records. Keep private states out of indexing. Respect the approved consent policy
and emit analytics only for actual successful actions.
