# Premium skincare art direction

Read `docs/design-brief.md` and `docs/asset-brief.md`. Begin with the product's real
packaging and the shopper's immediate task. Use Mineral Atelier as the preview
direction until a supplied brand identity replaces it.

Produce a compact visual contract: named palette, display/body type roles,
spacing scale, image treatment, hero composition, and separate mobile composition.
Show one memorable product scene surrounded by clear information and shopping
controls. Use a rendered mockup or screenshots when supported. Keep all product
labels, facts, and sample status legible.

## Design decisions

| Surface | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Product, approved benefit, price, size, primary action, initial image |
| Product page | Gallery, variant choice, delivery/policy information, evidence |
| Collection | Useful hierarchy, product comparison, clear available options |
| Mobile | Visible shopping action and natural vertical reading order |

Use spacing and material detail to create richness. Keep focus/hover behavior,
contrast, readable body copy, and touch targets deliberate. Draw imagery and
composition from the supplied product; remove decorative labels and repeated
entrance effects that add no information.

Build the static design first. Review at 390px, 768px, and 1440px; then revise
cropping, type scale, and action placement from actual screenshots. A screenshot
review complements keyboard and accessibility checks; it does not replace them.

Return the token/composition contract and the assets needed by frontend and
experience workers. Never invent reviews, clinical findings, or brand approvals.
