# Product content and discoverability

Read `docs/design-brief.md` and the product records before drafting. Separate real
brand-supplied facts from marked preview fixtures. Gather INCI ingredients, size,
usage, precautions, approved benefits, evidence references, image provenance,
shipping/returns facts, and selling markets in a product content record.

Use plain benefit-led copy and clear purchasing language. Preserve the actual
packaging wording where required. Mark unsupported claims for owner/qualified
review rather than publishing invented efficacy, clinical results, certifications,
disease treatment, or testimonials. Publish ratings only from genuine review data.

Generate canonical metadata, meaningful image descriptions, product/variant
structured data, sitemap, and robots behavior from shared content and commerce
records. Keep price/currency/availability synchronized. Exclude private cart and
account states from indexing. Distinguish preview/noindex settings from live SEO.

Define view_item, add_to_cart, and begin_checkout analytics using stable variant
references, provider currency, and no personal data. Emit events once for the
actual successful action. Do not emit checkout or purchase events based on a demo
button or failed mutation. Apply the market's approved consent policy before
nonessential trackers; never infer a completed purchase from a redirect alone.

Validate structured data, missing/duplicate products, canonical links, fixture
labels, policy links, event duplication, and readable mobile content. Keep legal
policy drafts and cosmetic evidence review as explicit business inputs rather
than asserting that another brand's wording guarantees compliance.

Return the content record, missing evidence, SEO mapping, and observed validation.
